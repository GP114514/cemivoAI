LONGER INTRODUCTION
cemivoAI explores software whose entire interface is driven by AI. The screen you interact with is a generated image: its controls, text, and changes follow your clicks and instructions. The system reads the whole frame, infers your intent, and chooses whether to redraw a small area or the full screen. A visual model trained from random weights produces the interface pixels, while an Agent connects those interactions to real tasks. This demonstration uses actual model outputs, measured local edits, and a document that was saved and read back to verify its content. The goal is one adaptive interface for many kinds of work. It remains a research prototype, with general UI quality still improving.
